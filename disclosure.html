Disclosure

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

That sentence is the whole Associates relationship. This page is the longer version, including what is not live yet.

Amazon Associates

Stoopden Guides is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. If you follow a live Amazon link from this site and buy, Amazon may pay a commission, at no extra cost to you.

On this prototype, Amazon product controls are placeholders labeled with ASIN_TODO_… IDs. They are disabled and do not go to Amazon. There is no Associates tag in the URLs yet. When links go live, this disclosure still applies.

The shop is separate

Purchases on stoopden.com are ordinary store checkout, not Amazon Associates. Shipping, returns, and order chat for those orders stay with the shop. Transit times vary; tracking posts once a carrier has the package. Shipping cost, if any, is whatever checkout shows.

Editorial

Guides are written as category analysis. We do not sell a review, invent star ratings, or fabricate ASINs to look complete. A disabled “Amazon link coming” control means the slot is waiting, not that a secret URL exists.

Some pages mention types of products that also appear in the Stoopden catalog. That is a topic overlap, not a claim that the Amazon slot and the shop SKU are the same item.

Other affiliate or referral links

If we add non-Amazon affiliate programs later, they will be named on this page before they go live. None are live on this prototype besides the Associates disclosure above.

Questions about an order: stoopden.com/pages/contact. Questions about this disclosure belong with the same chat, not a personal inbox on this site.
