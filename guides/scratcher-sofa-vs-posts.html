Buy the orientation your cat already uses on the real sofa.

Scratching is shoulder work, claw maintenance, and scent marking. It is not optional, and it is not a moral failing of the cat. In an apartment the question is which sacrificial object they will accept instead of the sofa you actually sit on.

At a glance

  • Helps Matching the sofa crime scene.
  • Skip The opposite orientation they already ignore.
  • Measure Horizontal versus vertical, then location.

Read the crime scene

Horizontal damage along a cushion edge usually means a cat that rakes forward on a low surface. Vertical damage up the arm means a cat that stands and pulls down. Some cats do both. If you only buy one shape, buy the dominant one.

Location matters as much as material. Cats often scratch near where they sleep or near the doorway they patrol. A post in the “pet corner” that nobody walks through is a post they will ignore.

Cardboard sofas

Double-layer cardboard sofas work because they are the right height for a reclined rake and because they feel like an object the cat is allowed to ruin. They shed. You will vacuum corrugate crumbs. That is the trade for a cheap, replaceable surface.

If the sofa is also a nap spot, put it where a bed might have gone — not in the traffic lane between kitchen and door.

Posts

A post has to be taller than a half-stretch and heavy enough that a full pull does not walk it across laminate. Lightweight posts teach the cat that the real sofa is more stable, which is the opposite lesson.

Sisal should be wrapped tight. Loosely wound rope becomes a pulling toy, then a swallowed fiber problem.

Protecting the real sofa

Put the scratcher between the cat and the damaged corner, not across the room. A temporary throw or a sticky deterrent on the stolen spot can buy time while the new object picks up scent. Catnip on the scratcher is optional; location and orientation do more work.

If the cat still prefers upholstery, you likely bought the wrong axis. Swap sofa-for-post (or the reverse) before you buy a bigger version of the thing they already ignored.
