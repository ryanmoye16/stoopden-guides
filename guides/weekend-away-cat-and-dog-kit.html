A weekend away is two kits: one that travels, one that stays.

How the weekend splits

Our separate guides cover each half on its own — the walk water-bottle kit guide and the apartment litter and travel guide. This guide is about the weekend itself, where the two halves fail in opposite ways: the walk kit fails when it stays on the hook by the door, and the litter corner fails when someone tries to improve it right before leaving.

So the rule is boring on purpose. What travels must already live on the leash. What stays must already be the setup the cat uses every day, left clean. A weekend is not the moment for a new box, a new bottle, or a new routine for whoever is checking in — it is the moment the existing routine has to survive without you.

When this kit is wrong

Skip the whole framing for a cat that will only go at home if the plan was to take the cat along — our litter guide says it plainly: practice with any travel tray at home first, and if they refuse, you need a plan with more stops, not a stronger air freshener. Skip the bottle as a weekend supply for the dog: it is a walk kit, and a long hike or a full day out needs more water than it carries. And if nobody is checking in at all, no litter corner fixes that — the kit assumes a daily look, the same way the feeding station does.

FAQ

What should stay set up for a cat when you go away for a weekend?

The litter corner the cat already uses, left clean: the hooded corner pan scooped with fresh litter, its mat shaken out and fully dry underneath, in the corner it already lives in. A weekend is not the time to move the box or swap in a new one — a cat that will only go at home needs the familiar setup, and whoever checks in needs a corner that is easy to scoop, not a new system to learn.

Can one walk bottle cover a whole weekend with a small dog?

No, and it is not meant to. The dual-chamber bottle in this kit suits neighborhood walks, not long hikes or a weekend supply — it carries water and dry food for the walks themselves. Refill it daily, empty and wash it when you get back, and treat the home bowl as the dog’s real water station.

Should you set up a new litter box the morning you leave?

No. Folding boxes leak and disposable trays tip, and a cat surprised with a new cave on the morning of a drive may simply refuse it. If a trip needs a different box, practice with it at home first. For a normal weekend, the honest kit is the corner pan and mat the cat already knows, left clean.
