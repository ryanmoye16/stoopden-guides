Stoopden is everyday pet gear for home and on the go. These guides follow five hubs — feed, play, groom, rest, travel — for US cats and small dogs, especially in apartments. We are not a general pet marketplace and we are not writing for giant breeds or farm gear.

At a glance

  • Helps Small homes, cats and small dogs.
  • Skip Invented products and star averages.
  • Measure Footprint, noise, a sink you will actually use.

What a guide is

A guide is original commentary: what the object is for, who should skip it, what to measure, and how it fails in a small floor plan. It is not a scraped grid of logos. It is not a list of star ratings. It is not a rewrite of a product listing.

stoopden.com is this guide site. There is no catalog checkout here. When Amazon Associates links go live, those purchases happen on Amazon and stay labeled as affiliate links.

Audience filters

  • Fits a small home (footprint, noise, wash-in-a-small-sink).
  • Useful to cats, small dogs, or both — not a giant-breed assumption.
  • Daily life: feeding, play, grooming, rest, walking, litter. Not costume accessories.
  • US shipping context when we talk about getting gear at all. We do not publish delivery-day promises.

How product slots work

Each recommended type of object gets a criteria card. Editors keep an ASIN_TODO_… ID in the HTML. Until a real ASIN and Associates tag are filled in:

  • The card is a finished hold: criteria only, no invented listing.
  • The control reads Link when we name one.
  • The control is disabled. There is no Amazon URL to click.
  • We do not invent prices, review counts, star averages, or ASINs.

What we will not write

  • Fabricated testimonials or “readers say” quotes.
  • Free-shipping promises. Shipping belongs to the seller and the checkout you actually use.
  • Personal inboxes on this site.
  • Medical claims. Joint ramps and water fountains are comfort and maintenance topics, not treatment.

How a page gets updated later

  1. Choose a real ASIN that matches the slot’s type-level criteria.
  2. Add the Associates tag in one place (see the README). Do not sprinkle one-off tags.
  3. Turn the disabled control into a real link, still with the disclosure line on the page.
  4. Keep the commentary. Do not replace the essay with a spec sheet.

If we cannot yet name a product honestly, the card stays a criteria hold. That is the finished version of this site.
