About

Short guides for a small home.

Stoopden is everyday pet gear for home and on the go. This site is the guide layer: how to think about feed, play, groom, rest, and travel in a small US home.

Who it is for

Cats and small dogs, especially in apartments. People who want fewer, better objects — a feeder that can be washed in a real sink, a scratcher that matches how the cat already wrecks the sofa, a ramp that still leaves a hallway.

If you need giant-breed gear, this is the wrong den.

What this site is

stoopden.com is Stoopden Guides: static pages on GitHub Pages. There is no catalog checkout here. Amazon product slots stay empty until a real ASIN is filled in. They are not a second storefront.

Who writes this

Commentary is original analysis of the category — what to measure, what fails in a small floor plan, what to skip. It is not a dump of marketplace listings. Product buttons stay off until a real ASIN is filled in. Read how we pick.
